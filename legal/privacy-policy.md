# Loop — Privacy Policy and Terms of Use

**Last updated:** July 28, 2026
**Version:** 1.0
**Contact for data requests:** ahadihjosue@gmail.com

---

## 1. Who we are

Loop is a platform that connects cargo owners with nearby verified vehicle drivers in Rwanda. Loop provides the matching service only. It is not a transport operator and does not carry goods. The contract of carriage is between the cargo owner and the driver.

## 2. What we collect

- **Account information:** your name, phone number and email address.
- **Driver verification documents:** driving licence, national identity document and vehicle registration, collected only from users registering as drivers.
- **Location:** your device's coordinates. For drivers, this is collected while you are set to online, so that nearby jobs can be matched to you. For cargo owners, it is collected when you post a job, to establish the pickup point.
- **Job information:** pickup and drop-off points, cargo description, weight, vehicle type and price.
- **Messages** exchanged with your counterparty about a job.
- **Ratings** you give and receive.
- **Payment records:** the amount, the time, and the reference issued by our payment provider. We do not collect or store your card number, PIN or mobile-money credentials.

## 3. Why we collect it and on what basis

Account information identifies you and lets your counterparty contact you about a job. Verification documents establish that a driver is licensed and that the vehicle is registered, which is the basis on which cargo owners trust the platform. Location makes matching possible; without it the service cannot function. Messages and job records provide a record of what was agreed. Ratings allow each side to build a reputation.

We process this information to provide the service you have asked for, and to meet our obligations under Law No. 058/2021 relating to the protection of personal data and privacy.

## 4. Who we share it with

Your counterparty on a job sees your name, your rating, your vehicle type where you are a driver, and your approximate location. They do not see your verification documents.

We also use the following service providers, which process data on our behalf:

- **Flutterwave** — payment processing. Your card or mobile-money details are entered on Flutterwave's own page and are never transmitted to or stored by Loop.
- **Africa's Talking** — delivery of SMS notifications to drivers.
- **OpenStreetMap and OSRM** — route and distance calculation, and address lookup.
- **Railway** — hosting of our servers and database.

Some of these providers operate outside Rwanda, so your information may be processed abroad. We do not sell your personal data to anyone, and we do not share it for advertising.

## 5. Verification documents

Verification documents receive stricter handling than other data. They are visible only to authorised Loop administrators reviewing a verification request, every access and decision is recorded in an audit log, and they are never shown to cargo owners or to other drivers.

Deleting your account (section 8) erases these documents. Deleting them ends your eligibility to be matched as a driver, so you would need to re-verify to drive again.

## 6. Location data

As a driver, your location is used to match you to nearby jobs while you are online. Setting yourself offline stops your location being used for matching.

Verification and availability are separate states. Being approved does not set you online or make you visible. When your verification is approved we tell you that you are verified and invite you to go online; you appear to cargo owners only once you choose to go online from the app, which is the moment your device shares your current location. We never mark you online on your behalf, because showing you as available without your live location would be dishonest to both you and the cargo owner. An administrator can set you offline, but cannot set you online, for the same reason.

## 7. Ratings, suspension and how to contest them

Ratings are mutual. After a completed job, each party may rate the other, and your average rating is visible to users deciding whether to work with you.

An administrator may set a driver offline, or suspend an account where our terms have been breached. A suspended account cannot sign in and a suspended driver is excluded from matching. Every such action is logged with the administrator responsible and the time it was taken.

If you believe a rating is unfair or a suspension mistaken, contact us at the address above. We will review the decision and respond within 5 working days.

## 8. How long we keep it and how to delete it

We keep the information described in section 2 for as long as your account is open. We do not delete it automatically on a schedule; instead we make deletion available to you, which is what Law No. 058/2021 requires. What each category holds while your account is open:

- Account information: kept while your account is open.
- Verification documents: kept while your account is open, so you remain matchable as a driver. The document files are held in private storage, separate from your account record.
- Location: we store only your most recent position, not a history or trail. Each update overwrites the previous one; going offline stops it being used for matching.
- Messages and job records: kept while your account is open, as the record of what was agreed with your counterparty.
- Payment references: the amount, time and provider reference for a settlement (see section 4). These are financial records and are treated separately from your account (see below).

You can delete your account yourself, at any time, from the app, after confirming your password. You may also ask us to delete it using the contact address above, and we will action the request within 5 working days.

When an account is deleted, we erase your account information and the records that belong only to you: your posted jobs, your proposals, the ratings you received, and, for drivers, your vehicle details and verification document files.

Some records involve another person. Where a record is shared, such as a conversation or a rating you gave a counterparty, we do not delete the other person's copy. Instead we remove your identity from it: your messages remain in the other party's conversation, shown as from a deleted user, and a rating you gave stays part of that person's reputation with your name removed. Their score is recalculated so it stays accurate.

Two further things are treated differently, and we tell you so plainly:

- **Payment records are kept, with your identity removed.** Because a settlement is a financial record, we retain the amount, time and provider reference, but we remove the link to you and delete the provider's raw response. Once de-identified in this way the record is no longer personal data, and we keep it indefinitely as a financial record. It can no longer be traced back to you.
- **Removal for false documents leaves a minimal block.** If a driver is removed for submitting false documents (see section 10), we keep a one-way fingerprint of the email address and phone number used (not the values themselves) so the same details cannot immediately be used to register again. Nothing else about the account is retained.

## 9. Your rights

Under Law No. 058/2021 you may:

- request a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
- ask us to correct anything inaccurate;
- ask us to delete your data;
- object to particular processing;
- complain to the national supervisory authority.

Contact us using the address above and we will respond within 5 working days.

## 10. Terms of use

- You must be at least 18 years old to use Loop.
- You must provide accurate information. Drivers must submit genuine documents; submitting false documents will result in permanent removal.
- Loop matches cargo owners with drivers and does not transport goods. The agreement to carry is between you and your counterparty, and Loop is not a party to it.
- Loop does not hold your money. Payments pass directly to the driver through our payment provider, and Loop never holds funds between you and your counterparty.
- The price shown when a job is posted is an estimate produced by our pricing model, not a regulated tariff. The final price is what the two parties agree.
- You may not use Loop to move goods you are not lawfully entitled to move, harass another user, or attempt to access another user's account or data.
- You may close your account at any time.

## 11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and notify you in the application before the change takes effect.
